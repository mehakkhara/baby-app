Photo-powered interactivity 📸

Five ways to turn the journal photos she's already saving into moments she plays with, not just scrolls past. Placeholder gradients stand in for real photos — every demo is tappable/draggable.

💡 Why photos are the right lever: they're already on-device in IndexedDB, so every feature here is $0 — no uploads, no processing costs, no APIs. And each feature quietly rewards adding more photos, which feeds the flashback card and the journal itself. It's a flywheel.

A · Then ↔ Now slider

Picks the oldest + newest journal photo automatically · pure CSS clip-path

A draggable comparison of the earliest photo in the journal against the latest. Babies transform so fast that this is genuinely astonishing to a parent — and dragging the handle back and forth is the kind of tactile play that makes an app feel alive. Smarter pairing later: same-pose matching by month ("Month 1 vs Month 4").

every new photo she adds updates "Now" — the comparison literally grows with the baby, so it's never the same twice.
Journal header, and on Home the day after a new photo is added ("See how far Aria's come").

C · Monthly photo hunt

9 age-aware prompts per month · a curated list per age band, like your tips

A 3×3 card of tiny photo missions for this month — "tiny toes", "mid-yawn", "bath time", "with grandma". Tapping a cell opens the camera/uploader; the photo fills the cell and saves to the journal (tagged with the prompt as its note). Prompts rotate with the baby's age: month 6 gets "first taste of solids", month 10 gets "standing up".

this is the strongest photo retention loop — an unfinished grid is a gentle open-ended quest that spans the whole month, and it directly solves "what should I even photograph?" It also feeds every other feature here with better, more varied photos.
Its own card on Home below the memory book; completed grids archive into the Journal as a monthly keepsake.

What I'd build first

Suggested order:
  1. Then ↔ Now slider (A) — smallest build, biggest emotional hit, works as soon as she has 2 photos.
  2. Monthly photo hunt (C) — the retention engine. It generates the photo supply that makes A, B, and E shine.
  3. Milestone keepsake cards (D) — rides on the existing milestone-photo feature; adds the share loop.
  4. Timelapse reel (B) — needs 3+ months of photos to feel magical; ship when the library has depth.
  5. Collage poster (E) — month-end companion to the weekly recap; nice-to-have once the rest lands.

All five read from the journal's IndexedDB store — no new storage, no server, no cost. One shared bit of plumbing helps all of them: a tiny helper that indexes photos by baby-age-month (you already have groupByMonth — extract it from JournalScreen into a lib).
